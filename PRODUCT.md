# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Casual learners and space-curious people looking for a short, approachable knowledge quiz.

## Product Purpose

Orbit Check offers a ten-question space exploration quiz. Success means answering questions, seeing clear feedback and progress, and understanding the final score.

## Positioning

A quick, single-question-at-a-time space quiz pairs immediate explanatory feedback with a mission-control framing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Ten multiple-choice questions with score tracking, progress, answer feedback, and a results screen.
- Runs as a single `index.html` file with no server or external dependencies.
- Supports system light and dark themes.
- Preserve the existing quiz flow and mission-control selection states when making changes.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the name Orbit Check.
- Use concise, approachable space-exploration language with mission-control cues.
- Preserve both light and dark themes.

## Product Principles

- Make the question, answer choices, progress, and score immediately clear.
- Give prompt, accurate feedback and a useful explanation for each answer.
- Keep the quiz lightweight and self-contained.
- Make every interaction usable with a keyboard and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, announced feedback and results, and reduced-motion support.
